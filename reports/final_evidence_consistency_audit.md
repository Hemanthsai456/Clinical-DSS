## 6. Discrepancies, Limitations, and Risk Analysis

1. **Boundary Mismatch in Neural Span Extraction:**
   Out of 2,637 relaxed True Positives achieved by the 3-epoch Colab transformer, **1,021 spans (38.72%) exhibited boundary mismatches** (e.g. predicting subword boundaries like `" chronic"` instead of gold `"chronic"`). While relaxed F1 reaches **0.8330**, exact F1 is **0.5105**.
2. **Clinical Dataset Vintage (1999–2008):**
   The UCI diabetes cohort reflects clinical practices from two decades ago. Inpatient treatment guidelines, sliding-scale insulin standards, and post-discharge outreach have evolved.
3. **Censoring of Out-of-System Readmissions:**
   The negative class in UCI includes `NO`, meaning "no readmission recorded within these 130 hospitals." Encounters resulting in readmission to unparticipating regional hospitals, VA centers, or community clinics outside the consortium are uncaptured.
4. **Historical Test-Set Exposure:**
   Both the UCI test set and the Synth-SBDH reviewed test set were evaluated during earlier milestones to generate baseline artifacts. These sets must be treated as frozen historical benchmarks, not untouched prospective validation sets.
